玩

怎么玩？

选一首歌，点 ▶。发光的小鱼干落到猫头上的线时，点那只猫下面。点中了，那只猫就唱出这个音。

怎么选自己控制哪几只猫？

开始前点猫。1 到 6 只都可以，选得越多越难，其余的猫自己唱。

按不准会怎样？

没有失败。按漏了，猫也会接着唱完这首歌。

可以只听不玩吗？

可以。让六只猫自己把整首唱完，当作播放器来听。

怎么撸猫？

听歌或者玩的时候，点一下或者长按猫咪。

声音和画面对不上？

打开设置，调「音画同步」，提前或延后一点，找到顺手的位置。戴蓝牙耳机时常常要往后调。

手机静音了还有声音？

是的。喵卡贝拉像音乐 App 一样出声，静音开关不会关掉猫的歌声；猫开始唱时，别的 App 正在放的音乐会暂停。想小声一点，按音量键。

只能横屏吗？

是的，手机横过来玩。

要联网吗？

不用。也不用注册、登录。

MIDI 和谱面图

怎么用自己的 MIDI？

在首页的歌单里选「＋ 导入」，再选一个 MIDI 文件。猫会先学一遍，自己分好声部。点 ✎ 进温泉点歌本，可以把音给别的猫、挪高挪低、写音、试听，改了会自己存好。

怎么把自己导入的歌存成谱面图？

在歌单里选中这首歌，点分享的符号，谱面机就出来了。签个名（可以不填），勾上那句确认，按「印」，再点「存到相册」。

怎么读回谱面图？

在歌单里选「＋ 导入」，再选这张图。也可以直接拍下屏幕上显示的谱面图。

谱面图读不出来？

请用原图。被压得很小、裁过、加了滤镜的图可能读不出来。拍照时让整张图都在画面里，别反光。

「一张谱面图装不下」？

这首太长或音太多了。在点歌本里剪下一段，或者擦掉一些音。

存档

存档在哪？换手机会带过去吗？

存档只在这台设备上，不会自己跟到新手机。删掉游戏，存档也就没了。

Playing

How do I play?

Pick a song and tap ▶. When a glowing fish reaches the line over a cat's head, tap below that cat. A hit makes that cat sing the note.

How do I choose which cats I play?

Tap the cats before you start. Anywhere from 1 to 6: the more you pick, the harder it gets. The other cats sing on their own.

What happens if I miss?

Nothing bad. There is no fail: the cats keep singing to the end of the song.

Can I just listen?

Yes. Let the six cats sing the whole song by themselves and use it as a music player.

How do I pet the cats?

While listening or playing, tap or hold a cat.

The sound and the picture don't line up.

Open Settings and move "Timing" earlier or later until it feels right. Bluetooth headphones often need it later.

Why is there sound with my phone on silent?

Meowcappella plays sound the way a music app does: the silent switch doesn't mute the cats, and music playing in another app pauses when the cats start singing. Use the volume buttons to make it quieter.

Landscape only?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways.

Do I need to be online?

No. No sign-up or sign-in either.

MIDI and chart pictures

How do I use my own MIDI?

In the song list on the home screen, choose "+ Import", then a MIDI file. The cats learn it first and share out the parts by themselves. Tap ✎ to open the Songbook: hand notes to another cat, move them up or down, write notes, listen. Changes save on their own.

How do I save a song I imported as a chart picture?

Pick the song in the song list, tap the share symbol and the Chartomat appears. Sign it (optional), tick the confirmation box, press Print, then "Save to Photos".

How do I read a chart picture back in?

In the song list choose "+ Import", then the picture. You can also photograph a chart picture shown on a screen.

The chart picture won't read.

Use the original picture. Pictures that were shrunk, cropped or filtered may not read. When taking a photo, keep the whole picture in frame and avoid glare.

"Too much for one chart picture"?

The song is too long or has too many notes. In the Songbook, cut out one part or erase some notes.

Saves

Where are my saves? Do they move to a new phone?

Saves stay on this device and don't follow you to a new one. Deleting the game deletes them.
