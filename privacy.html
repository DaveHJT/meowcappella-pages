隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 22 日 · 适用于 iPhone 版、电脑版和 TapTap 版

《喵卡贝拉》不联网，不收集你的任何信息。你的存档只在你自己的设备上。

我们不收集什么

  • 游戏不联网。所有内容都在安装包里，玩的时候不向我们或任何第三方发送数据。
  • 不用注册，不用登录。不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、设备标识、位置。
  • 没有广告，没有统计或追踪工具，没有第三方 SDK。

存在你设备上的东西

  • 设置（语言、音画同步等）、每首歌的最高分。
  • 你导入或改过的谱面，谱面机上填的署名。
  • 电脑版另有桌面设置：壁纸、白噪音、打字猫是谁、音量、开机启动。

存在哪：iPhone 版在 App 自己的数据里；电脑版在 %APPDATA%\喵卡贝拉\；TapTap 版在游戏页面自己的本地存储里。

删掉游戏（电脑版：删掉上面那个文件夹）就全部清除。这些东西我们拿不到，也帮不了你恢复。

相机（只在 iPhone 版）

只在「导入谱面图」时，你在系统的选择器里点了「拍照」，才会用相机，用来拍下别人的谱面图。照片只在本机读成谱面，游戏不保存这张照片。

照片（只往里存）

你点「存到相册」时，游戏把谱面图存进你的照片。它只能往里存，不能看你的照片。

你选的文件

导入 MIDI 或谱面图时，游戏只读你选中的那一个文件，用来生成谱面。

打字猫（只在电脑版）

打字猫要知道你按了键，才能跟着动。它只收到「按了一下」，不知道按的是哪个键。你打的内容不记录、不保存、不发送。随时可以收起它：点一下猫，在冒出来的值班牌上点「收起」，或者按 Ctrl+Alt+M。

动态壁纸（只在电脑版）

鼠标停在桌面上时，壁纸用光标的位置让猫看向它；你点桌面时，用点的位置让最近的那只猫应一声。这些位置只在那一刻用，不保存、不发送。

平台

  • iPhone 版从 App Store 下载，下载和付款由 Apple 处理。
  • 电脑版从 Steam 安装和更新。如果你开了 Steam 云存档，存档文件由 Steam 按它自己的规则同步。
  • TapTap 版运行在 TapTap 里，TapTap 的账号、防沉迷等服务按 TapTap 的隐私政策。

这些平台怎么处理你的信息，请看它们各自的隐私政策。

儿童

游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

以后有变化

政策改了，会更新这一页和上面的日期。以后如果加了要新权限的功能，会先写在这里，并在第一次用到时问你。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Covers the iPhone, PC and TapTap versions

Meowcappella works offline and collects nothing about you. Your saves stay on your own device.

What we don't collect

  • The game does not go online. Everything is in the install package, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else while you play.
  • No sign-up, no sign-in. We don't collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, device identifiers or location.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Settings (language, timing and so on) and your best score for each song.
  • Charts you import or edit, and the name you sign on the Chartomat.
  • On PC, the desktop settings too: wallpaper, ambience, which cat is typing, volume, start with Windows.

Where: on iPhone, in the app's own data; on PC, in %APPDATA%\喵卡贝拉\; on TapTap, in the game page's own local storage.

Deleting the game (on PC: deleting that folder) removes all of it. We can't see it and can't restore it for you.

Camera (iPhone only)

The camera is used only when you import a chart picture and choose "Take Photo" in the system picker, to photograph someone else's chart picture. The photo is read into a chart on your device; the game doesn't keep it.

Photos (add only)

When you tap "Save to Photos", the game adds the chart picture to your photos. It can add pictures but can't see your photos.

Files you choose

When you import a MIDI file or a chart picture, the game reads only the file you picked, to make a chart from it.

Typing cat (PC only)

The typing cat needs to know you pressed a key so it can move along. It only gets "a key was pressed", never which key. What you type is not recorded, stored or sent. Put it away any time: click the cat and choose "Put away" on its duty tag, or press Ctrl+Alt+M.

Live wallpaper (PC only)

When the pointer rests on the desktop, the wallpaper uses its position so the cats can look at it; when you click the desktop, it uses that spot so the nearest cat answers. These positions are used in the moment only, never stored or sent.

Platforms

  • The iPhone version comes from the App Store; Apple handles downloads and payment.
  • The PC version is installed and updated through Steam. If you turn on Steam Cloud, Steam syncs the save file under its own rules.
  • The TapTap version runs inside TapTap; TapTap's account, play-time and other services follow TapTap's privacy policy.

For how these platforms handle your information, see their own privacy policies.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. If a future feature needs a new permission, we will describe it here first and ask you the first time it is used.
