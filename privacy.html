隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日 · 适用于 iPhone 版

《喵卡贝拉》不联网，不收集你的任何信息。你的存档只在你自己的手机上。

我们不收集什么

  • 游戏不联网。所有内容都在安装包里，玩的时候不向我们或任何第三方发送数据。
  • 不用注册，不用登录。不收集姓名、手机号、邮箱、设备标识、位置。
  • 没有广告，没有统计或追踪工具，没有第三方 SDK。
  • 不用相机，不用麦克风。

存在你手机上的东西

  • 设置（语言、音画同步等）、每首歌的最高分。
  • 你导入或改过的谱面，谱面机上填的署名。

这些都在 App 自己的数据里。删掉 App 就全部清除。我们拿不到，也帮不了你恢复。

你选的照片和文件

导入 MIDI 或谱面图时，你在系统的选择器里选一张照片或一个文件，游戏只读你选中的那一个，用来生成谱面。游戏看不到你没选的照片和文件。

存到相册（只往里存）

你点「存到相册」时，游戏把谱面图存进你的照片。它只能往里存，不能看你的照片。

App Store

游戏从 App Store 下载，下载和付款由 Apple 处理，按 Apple 的隐私政策。

儿童

游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

以后有变化

政策改了，会更新这一页和上面的日期。以后如果加了要新权限的功能，会先写在这里，并在第一次用到时问你。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Covers the iPhone version

Meowcappella works offline and collects nothing about you. Your saves stay on your own phone.

What we don't collect

  • The game does not go online. Everything is in the install package, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else while you play.
  • No sign-up, no sign-in. We don't collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, device identifiers or location.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party SDKs.
  • No camera, no microphone.

What stays on your phone

  • Settings (language, timing and so on) and your best score for each song.
  • Charts you import or edit, and the name you sign on the Chartomat.

All of this is in the app's own data. Deleting the app removes all of it. We can't see it and can't restore it for you.

Photos and files you choose

When you import a MIDI file or a chart picture, you pick one photo or file in the system picker, and the game reads only that one to make a chart from it. The game can't see photos or files you didn't pick.

Save to Photos (add only)

When you tap "Save to Photos", the game adds the chart picture to your photos. It can add pictures but can't see your photos.

App Store

The game comes from the App Store; Apple handles downloads and payment under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. If a future feature needs a new permission, we will describe it here first and ask you the first time it is used.
